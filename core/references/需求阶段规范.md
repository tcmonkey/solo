# Intake and requirements standard

## Goal

Establish the business problem and testable scope before designing a solution.

## Intake behavior

Summarize the user's inputs as:

- understood background and current behavior;
- target users and their problem;
- intended outcome and success signal;
- constraints and dependencies;
- evidence-backed facts;
- assumptions;
- conflicts and open questions.

If material information is missing, ask one consolidated question containing only decisions whose answers change scope or acceptance. Optional material may be requested separately: screenshots, current flows, API samples, data examples, design references or existing documents.

## Requirements artifact

Adapt `assets/01 需求文档.md`. Define:

- goals as measurable outcomes where possible;
- non-goals to prevent accidental scope growth;
- functional requirements as `REQ-F-###`;
- non-functional requirements as `REQ-NF-###`;
- constraints as `CON-###`;
- acceptance criteria using observable Given/When/Then or equivalent statements;
- open questions as `Q-###` with owner and impact;
- assumptions as `ASM-###` with a validation method.

Avoid embedding a preferred implementation in a requirement unless technology is itself a constraint. Do not invent market data, business rules or user preferences.

## Scope gate quality bar

- Every in-scope behavior has an acceptance criterion.
- Normal, empty, error, permission and boundary behavior are addressed or explicitly deferred.
- Goals and non-goals do not conflict.
- High-impact assumptions are confirmed or clearly block the affected work.
- Each source-backed requirement points to at least one `SRC-###`.
