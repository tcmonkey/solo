# Product solution standard

## Goal

Describe how users and operators experience the solution without prematurely fixing internal implementation details.

## Product artifact

Adapt `assets/02 产品方案.md`. Include:

- solution summary and principles;
- target personas and scenarios;
- information architecture or domain navigation;
- primary and alternate user flows;
- feature behavior mapped to requirement IDs;
- business rules and validations;
- loading, empty, success, partial, error and recovery states;
- roles and permissions;
- notification and copy behavior where relevant;
- analytics events and success measurement;
- compatibility and accessibility expectations;
- release scope, deferred scope and dependencies.

Use `FLOW-###`, `RULE-###`, and `EVT-###` identifiers. Prefer tables for repeated state behavior and mappings.

## Product gate quality bar

- Every `REQ-F-###` is covered or explicitly rejected with a reason.
- A user can complete each primary scenario end to end.
- Failure, retry, cancellation and duplicate-action behavior are defined where relevant.
- Permission and data-visibility differences are explicit.
- The product specification remains verifiable without reading the technical design.
